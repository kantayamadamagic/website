Biography

Takumi Kuroda is an actor based in Tokyo, known for his naturalistic performances in film and television drama.

After studying theater and performance, he began his professional career on stage before moving into film and television. His performances are characterized by a restrained presence and a strong attention to character detail.

He joined YAMADA PRODUCTION in 2022 and has since appeared in independent films, television dramas, and commercial projects.
